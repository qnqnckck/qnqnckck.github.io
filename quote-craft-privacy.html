QuoteCraft Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: August 12, 2026 · App ID: com.labhub.quotecraft

QuoteCraft is a local-first app with no account or developer backend. The developer does not receive the business records you enter.

Data stored on device

Pricing inputs, business profile, clients, service items, quotes, invoices, payments, hours, costs, language, theme, cached Pro entitlement, and settings are stored in app-private storage on your device.

Export and deletion

If you export JSON or share a PDF, the operating-system share sheet and the selected destination's policies apply. You can delete individual records in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data. Exported files must be deleted at their destination.

In-app purchases

Google Play Billing or Apple App Store processes Pro purchases and restoration. The developer does not receive card numbers. The store may process account, transaction, and device data under its own privacy policy.

Ads, analytics, and children

QuoteCraft contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK. It is a business tool for freelancers and is not designed for children.
