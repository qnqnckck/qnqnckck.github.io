Privacy, kept simple.

App: Shape Escape: Puzzle Rescue · Bundle ID: com.labhub.shapeescape

Shape Escape processes only the information needed to provide the puzzle, optional advertising, and purchases.

On-device data

No account is required. Level progress, settings, heart recovery state, and the cached purchase entitlement are stored in the app's private on-device storage. LABHUB does not collect this game data on a separate server.

Advertising

The free experience can display optional Google AdMob rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may process advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from an IP address for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, and measurement. Consent choices are requested where required. Unlimited Play users are not shown rewarded ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes the one-time Unlimited Play purchase. The app cannot access payment-card information and receives only product, transaction, purchase-state, and entitlement information provided by the store.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until reset or app deletion. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; LABHUB cannot recover it. Store purchase records remain subject to the store provider's policies and can be restored through the store account.

Children and security

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request names, messages, contacts, or precise location.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Questions may be sent to qnqnqnckck@gmail.com.
