Privacy

Effective: 2026-10-08

No account is required. Records, skins, items and settings stay on the device and are not sent to a developer server.

Ad-enabled versions use Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Advertising services may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Ads require internet access.

Consent forms and advertising privacy choices in Settings are provided where required. Core gameplay remains available without ad delivery.

The developer does not request contacts, photos or precise location. Support emails use your email address and message to answer your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Clear device app data or uninstall the app to remove local records. Operating-system backups follow device settings. Consult Google policies for advertising-service data.
