Support

Shape Escape: Puzzle Rescue is an offline-first spatial logic puzzle. No account or login is required.

How to play

Select a shape to display every valid movement arrow. Tap an indicated destination cell to move. Free all required shapes to complete the level. The level header shows your current progress out of 300.

Hearts, undo, and restart

A failed attempt or restart may consume a heart. Hearts recover over time. A rewarded ad can refill hearts when available, and Undo reverses a move so you can test a different route.

Unlimited Play

The one-time Unlimited Play purchase removes heart limits and rewarded ads and unlocks the full 300-level campaign. Use Restore Purchases on the same store account if your entitlement is missing.

Troubleshooting

Restart the app and update to the latest version. Purchases and ads need a connection, but the core puzzle boards and local progress remain available offline. Reinstalling the app may remove progress stored on the device.
