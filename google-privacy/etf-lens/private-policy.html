ETF Lens Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

ETF Lens is a local-first ETF/ETN discovery, comparison, and watchlist app.

Data stored on your device

  • Watchlist selections, comparison state, language, and theme preferences are stored locally.
  • Bundled market snapshots are included for offline use.
  • No account registration or brokerage credentials are required.
  • An optional Tiingo API token is stored in encrypted platform storage. ETF Lens does not store a Tiingo password.

Network use

  • The app may download permitted, operator-published ETF resources. Korean quote batches may use public Financial Services Commission data or approved KRX data. The app does not directly contact Yahoo.
  • ETF Lens does not intentionally attach a personal identifier to refresh requests.
  • If refresh fails, the latest cached or bundled snapshot remains available with freshness and source status.
  • Optional U.S. ETF requests go directly from the device to Tiingo. The token is sent only in the authorization header.

Advertising and data sharing

  • ETF Lens does not sell user data or transmit local watchlists, notes, or settings to a proprietary backend.
  • A limited Google Mobile Ads banner is shown in Settings. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, approximate location inferred from IP, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.
  • Where required, Google's consent form is shown before an ad request and advertising privacy choices can be reopened from Settings.
  • Advertising never changes rankings, scores, search results, or watchlist behavior.

Financial disclaimer

ETF Lens provides market information and organization tools only. It does not provide investment advice, suitability analysis, brokerage execution, or guaranteed-return recommendations.

Contact

For privacy or data-source questions, contact the publisher through the support channel shown in the app store listing.
