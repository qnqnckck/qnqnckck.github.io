Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Provider: LABHUB

Three Kingdoms: Dragon Oath

On-device information

Battle progress, settings and verified ad-removal status are stored on your device. No developer account or developer game server is required. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may delete local saves. Purchase restoration is not a backup of game progress.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and consent management. Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device/advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery and measurement, security, fraud prevention and service operation. Processing depends on consent, region and device settings. The game does not show interstitial ads after ad removal is purchased.

Purchases

Google Play handles Android payments and Apple App Store handles iOS payments. The app checks signed purchase information or StoreKit-verified transactions for ad removal. The developer does not receive card numbers or send purchase receipts to a developer server. Restore uses the purchasing account on the same store; the two stores have separate entitlements.

Choices and consent

Where applicable and available, advertising privacy options can be reopened in game settings. You can also use Android advertising privacy settings and your Google account settings. Core gameplay continues without an available ad or network connection.

iOS advertising privacy choices

The iOS version in preparation checks consent before ad requests and may request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission for the advertising identifier. Declining never limits gameplay; requests without authorization specify non-personalized ads. Use in-game ad privacy choices or iOS tracking settings. The SDK may process device identifiers, approximate location, app/ad interactions, crash data and performance data.

Retention, deletion and contact

You can delete local data by clearing app data or uninstalling. Information processed by Google and advertising partners follows their retention/deletion policies. Support emails include your email address and information voluntarily provided, used to resolve your request. Do not send unnecessary personal information. Privacy requests can be sent to the support email below.

Children and policy updates

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Guardians may contact support with concerns. Check the actual store age guidance and content rating. If processing changes, this policy and effective date will be updated.
