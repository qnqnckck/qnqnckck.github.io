Where is my saved progress?

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove local progress. Check that you are using the same app on the same device. There is no developer-hosted save recovery or automatic cross-device sync.

I purchased ad removal but still see ads.

Sign into the Google Play account used for the purchase, connect to the internet and select Restore purchases in game settings. The same account can restore ad removal on another device. Restoration does not restore battle saves.

Can I play offline?

Core gameplay uses on-device data and continues when no ad is available. New purchases and restoration need Google Play connectivity. Previously verified ad removal remains available offline.

When do ads appear?

Ads may appear between battles starting with continuation after the second victory, and after confirming a defeat retry from battle three. They do not appear during a battle. Purchasing ad removal removes interstitial ads.

Can I change battle presentation?

Battle scenes and playback speed can be adjusted in settings. Disabling scenes does not change the rules or combat results.

How do refunds work?

Eligibility and procedures follow Google Play policies. See the refund guide below. A confirmed refund or revoked purchase may change the ad-removal entitlement.
